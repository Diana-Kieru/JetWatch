# How to delete your JetWatch data

Effective date: 1 October 2026

JetWatch does not have accounts. There is no username, email, or password to delete, and there is no JetWatch profile stored in the cloud.

The only data JetWatch keeps is on your phone: flights you follow, the last map positions, cached flight details, and your notification choice.

## Remove one followed flight

1. Open JetWatch.
2. Go to the **Saved** tab.
3. Tap **Unfollow** on that flight.

That flight is removed from the phone. JetWatch does not keep a copy anywhere else.

## Delete everything the app stored

Either of these deletes the saved flights, cached aircraft, and cached flight details:

- Uninstall JetWatch.
- Or, on the phone: **Settings → Apps → JetWatch → Storage → Clear data** (wording can vary by phone).

Clearing data also turns alerts off, because the followed flights are gone. You can install the app again later. It will start empty.

## What JetWatch cannot delete

OpenSky and AeroDataBox receive the map area and the flight searches needed to load public flight data. JetWatch does not send them your name or an account id, and it cannot delete logs those companies may keep under their own policies.

If you created your own OpenSky or RapidAPI account in a browser, that account is separate from JetWatch. Close it with that service, not in the app.
