## What the app is

JetWatch shows aircraft positions and flight schedules from the OpenSky Network and AeroDataBox. It can save flights on your phone and notify you about flights you follow.

Flight data can be delayed, incomplete, or wrong. JetWatch is not a source for flying, dispatch, air traffic control, or any other operational decision. Do not rely on it for safety of life or property.

## No account

JetWatch does not create an account for you. Data you save stays on your phone until you unfollow a flight, clear the app's storage, or uninstall. See [How to delete your data](delete-account.md).
